There's a point where the heart-risk curve stops dropping — and it sits past the target most of us were given.

The number in this video: in 88,281 UK adults wearing motion trackers, the drop in heart-disease risk levelled off at 213 minutes of moderate activity a week (95% range 207–219) — about three and a half hours. Below that mark, more minutes tracked with lower risk; past it, the curve ran flat.

For context: the familiar weekly target in most guidelines is 150 minutes (a guideline number, not this study's) — the levelling-off point here sits above it.

Study: An et al., Front Endocrinol, 2026 — prospective cohort (UK Biobank), median 7.29 years of follow-up. An association, not proof of cause.
