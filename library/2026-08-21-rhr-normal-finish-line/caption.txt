Your watch hands you this number every morning. 'In range' was never the finish line.

The numbers in this video: in 12,232 young adults, every 10 bpm higher resting heart rate tracked with a 26% higher risk of dying from any cause — and in the study's second, longer cohort, an 18% higher risk of heart-disease death. A slope, not a switch: no safe side of 'normal'.

Study: Chen et al., Open Heart, 2026 — population-based prospective cohort, median 17.8 years of follow-up (27.6 in the validation cohort). An association, not proof of cause.

Watch the trend, not just the range.
