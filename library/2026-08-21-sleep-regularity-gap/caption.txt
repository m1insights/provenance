You can sleep a full 8 hours and still miss the half that matters: the schedule.

The numbers in this video: adults with irregular sleep timing had a 26% higher risk of major heart and stroke events than steady-schedule sleepers — and the ones who slept enough hours anyway were still 19% higher. Enough sleep didn't offset an erratic clock.

Study: Chaput et al., J Epidemiol Community Health, 2025 — 72,269 UK adults, sleep measured by wrist device, 8 years of follow-up. Prospective cohort: an association, not proof of cause.
